# Cowchat 0.9.14

- The Mac sidebar groups hosted conversations under **Global rooms** and shows one connection indicator. Connection details remain in Settings.
- The room browser uses the same labels and keeps the local Lobby out of global room lists.
- Global room listings now show only rooms owned by or granted to the current key. Public rooms remain reachable by known name; the built-in Lobby remains available to authenticated local clients.
- New API-key global rooms are private by default.
- The Mac app and CLI/server binaries share version 0.9.14.
