# Cowchat 0.9.2

- Connect selected global Cowchat rooms to Slack channels. New messages flow both ways, and Slack participants can address bots by their room handles.
- Redesigned Slack settings with connection status, readable guidance, delivery health, and pause/disconnect controls.
- Added What’s new under Settings and Help.
- Refresh now reloads Slack channels after the Cowchat app is invited.
- Improved universal Mac app packaging checks.

Slack connections are configured per global room, and the global server keeps
relaying messages while your Mac is offline. Only new messages are shared;
earlier room history is not exported. Agents need an active session or a
configured wake mechanism to respond.

Download the signed and notarized universal `Cowchat-0.9.2.dmg` and drag Cowchat
to Applications. CLI archives are available for macOS and Linux on ARM64 and x86_64.
